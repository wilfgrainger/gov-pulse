## Source discovery and ingestion

The GitHub Actions ingest does not pin an annual page as the permanent source contract. It:

1. fetches the rolling NHS England RTT publication page;
2. discovers the current annual RTT data page;
3. discovers the latest monthly statistical press notice PDF;
4. downloads the PDF with a bounded timeout;
5. converts it to text with `pdftotext -layout`;
6. parses the release date, observation month, national headline, long-wait thresholds, activity and treatment-function rows;
7. validates the payload with the same strict Worker contract used for public responses;
8. submits it through authenticated `/ingest`;
9. verifies the live `nhsStats` route against the current contract.

Permanent HTTP, discovery, conversion, parsing, date, range, reconciliation or schema failures stop the workflow. No old embedded value is substituted.

## Measurement boundary

The headline is a count of incomplete pathways, not a count of unique people. Some people have more than one pathway. NHS England separately estimates around 6.2 million unique patients for May 2026.

The national headline includes estimates for acute trusts that did not submit RTT data:

- Sheffield Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust (`RHQ`);
- Torbay and South Devon NHS Foundation Trust (`RA9`).

The treatment-function rows do not include those estimates. PULSE states this difference beside the table rather than presenting the two levels as perfectly additive.

## Treatment-function evidence

PULSE displays the eight largest incomplete-pathway treatment functions found in the same press notice, including their published within-18-week percentages. These rows are sorted by pathway volume after parsing. The contract requires at least five valid, unique treatment-function rows and rejects malformed percentages, counts or duplicates.

## Currentness and failure behaviour

- The freshness clock is the NHS England publication date, not workflow execution time.
- The accepted publication expires after 45 days.
- Re-running the workflow cannot make an old release current.
- Direct `/refresh?section=nhsStats` is blocked because this section is authenticated-ingest only.
- Malformed, legacy or expired section data returns HTTP 503 or 502 as appropriate.
- Invalid NHS data is removed from the combined dataset.
- The frontend fallback contains no NHS values and shows an unavailable state.
- The production canary rejects old `waitingTrend`, `waitingBySpecialty` and `lifeExpectancyTrend` shapes and rejects embedded A&E, GP-wait and workforce fields.

## Withdrawn evidence

The previous page mixed one scraped RTT waiting-list value with embedded measures from different publications and periods:

- A&E four-hour performance;
- GP waiting time;
- NHS workforce;
- male and female life expectancy;
- an embedded waiting-list trend;
- unsupported specialty average waits and patient counts.

Those measures are withdrawn from this release. They may return only through separate primary-source contracts with their own geography, observation date, unit, revisions and failure behaviour.

## Revision status

NHS England states that RTT data can be revised when providers submit corrections and that revision releases are generally periodic. PULSE retains this caveat and does not describe the monthly values as immutable.
