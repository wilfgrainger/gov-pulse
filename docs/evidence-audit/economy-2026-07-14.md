# GDP, labour market and receipts evidence audit — 14 July 2026

## Scope

This audit replaces three PULSE dashboards that mixed observations, forecasts and structurally different series. The repaired pages answer three narrower questions:

1. What does the latest ONS monthly GDP bulletin say about the latest month and latest three months?
2. What does the latest ONS UK labour-market bulletin report for employment, unemployment, inactivity and vacancies?
3. What does the latest ONS public-sector-finance bulletin report for monthly central government receipts and the comparable month a year earlier?

## Source contracts

| PULSE section | Stable official source | Accepted evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GDP | ONS `GDP monthly estimate, UK` rolling bulletin page | One complete bulletin edition containing release date, observation month, monthly real-GDP change and three-month real-GDP change |
| Employment | ONS `UK labour market` rolling bulletin page | One complete bulletin edition containing a common rolling period for employment, unemployment and inactivity, plus a separately labelled vacancy period |
| Government receipts | ONS `Public sector finances, UK` rolling bulletin page | One complete bulletin edition containing the bulletin month, central government receipts and the signed change from the comparable month a year earlier |

The Worker discovers the current edition from each stable landing page on every refresh cycle. No edition slug or claimed current observation is embedded in the production fallback.

## Retrieval and validation

Each connector:

1. fetches the stable ONS landing page;
2. discovers the first current bulletin-edition URL;
3. fetches that edition with a five-second timeout and bounded retry delays;
4. fails immediately on permanent HTTP errors;
5. parses the release date, reference period and required values;
6. validates period alignment and signed movement;
7. records the bulletin URL in the payload;
8. fails closed if any required evidence is absent or malformed.

Failed response bodies are cancelled before retrying. Retrieval freshness is monitored separately from statistical currentness:

- the Worker checks each rolling page every four hours;
- a cache record becomes operationally stale after 36 hours without a successful retrieval;
- the observation contract judges currentness from the bulletin release date;
- the payload preserves the actual observation period separately.

## Removed evidence

### GDP

Withdrawn from the current evidence page:

- embedded annual GDP history;
- an OBR forecast presented alongside actual observations;
- IMF/G7 per-capita comparisons;
- structural sector shares and values.

These measures use different sources, periods, price bases and revision schedules. They may return only as separately contracted actual or forecast series.

### Employment

Withdrawn from the current evidence page:

- private-versus-public employment totals;
- public-sector workforce categories;
- a hand-built annual employment trend;
- unverified totals for employed, unemployed and inactive people.

The repaired page keeps Labour Force Survey rates together and labels the vacancies survey period separately.

### Government receipts

Withdrawn from the current evidence page:

- a financial-year total presented as current monthly evidence;
- category-by-category tax estimates;
- tax-to-GDP history and forecast;
- an average-receipts-per-person calculation.

The repaired page shows only one monthly ONS central-government-receipts measure and its like-for-like annual comparison.

## Fallback and cache behaviour

The shared fallback contract contains no older economy values. It contains an explicit unavailable shape only. A legacy KV record is rejected even if its fetch timestamp appears fresh, and the first public request rebuilds it from the current official bulletin. If rebuilding fails, the Worker returns an error and the page displays an unavailable state.

## Production assurance

The live-feed canary requires the new official bulletin shapes and rejects all removed legacy fields. It also requires ONS provenance on the payload and registry. This remains within the existing single Cloudflare Worker and KV namespace.
