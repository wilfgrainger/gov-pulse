# Data and third-party licensing

The Apache License 2.0 in [LICENSE](./LICENSE) applies to the original application source and original documentation in this repository. It does not relicense material owned by a publisher, government department, font author or other third party.

public-data.org uses named primary publications and source-owned observations. Before reusing a downloaded dataset, chart, PDF, spreadsheet, logo, font or publisher text, check the terms at the direct source URL carried by the evidence record. Government material may be available under the Open Government Licence v3.0, except where a source states otherwise. YouGov, Oddschecker, IMF, OECD, SIPRI, WHO, World Bank, ONS, NHS England and other publishers retain their own terms and attribution requirements.

The generated section downloads include source attribution metadata where the source contract permits distribution. A public-data.org application licence is not permission to scrape, bulk-republish, imply publisher endorsement, or reuse a source's trademarks or copyrighted presentation. Missing or unclear reuse rights remain a reason to link to the primary source rather than redistribute its material.
